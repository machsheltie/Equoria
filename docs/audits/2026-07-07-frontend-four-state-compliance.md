## Root-cause pattern (why one doctrine beats 15 improvisations)

1. **`{ data, isLoading }` destructuring** (dropping `isError`) converts every fetch failure into a fabricated empty state: HorseMarketplace ×3, Messages ×4, Clubs ×3, Grooms/Riders/Trainers manage dashboards, Bank tx, Community ×4, HorseTrader, LeaderboardsPage summary, MyStable, ProfilePage/StableView secondaries.
2. **`onSuccess`-only mutation hooks** leave failed writes invisible: all of `useClubs`, `useGrooms`, `useRiders`, `useTrainers` staff muts, foal lifecycle muts in `useBreeding`, `useForum` create-thread/post.
3. **Fabricated literals on display paths**: ScoreProgressionPanel `+5` gains, breeding `|| 75` scores and hardcoded fees, `'Stallion'` default, `?? 0` money/stats, hardcoded `'5'`/badges.
